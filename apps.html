Featured app

Video Lens: Any video, in any language, as text you can read, search and quote.

Paste a video link, a playlist, a channel or a few search words. Video Lens returns the transcript in the video’s own language, a translation into the language you name, a summary, and subtitle files, one row per video. When a video has no captions, it transcribes the speech itself.

Video Lens 可将 YouTube、哔哩哔哩、抖音、TikTok、Instagram 和 Facebook Reels 上的视频转成文字：原文转录、翻译成您指定的语言、摘要和字幕文件，每行带时间戳。没有字幕的视频会自动语音转写。

What you get

  • Sources: YouTube videos, Shorts, playlists, channels and search words; TikTok; Instagram and Facebook Reels; Bilibili; Douyin; Vimeo.
  • Outputs: the transcript; a translation into more than 30 languages; a summary in more than 20; SRT and VTT subtitles; a timestamp for every line.
  • Every YouTube row also carries the video’s views, likes, date and length.
  • It shows the list of videos and the cost before anything runs.
  • A video with music and no speech is reported as such and not charged.

Can I get an English transcript of a Bilibili video, with timestamps?

Yes. Paste the Bilibili link and ask for English; every line comes with its start and end time.

What if the video has no captions?

It transcribes the speech. A clip with only music is reported as having no words.

Can it do a whole channel or playlist?

Yes, one row per video, and you see the list before it runs.

Why not use YouTube’s own API?

YouTube’s API returns caption files only for videos your own account owns. Video Lens reads any public video, and Bilibili and Douyin have no public transcript API at all.

Featured app

Audience Intel: Name the people you sell to. Get the creators they already watch, with a way to reach each one.

Describe your audience in your own words. Audience Intel searches YouTube, TikTok, Bilibili and Douyin with the phrases that audience uses, finds the creators it keeps landing on, ranks them, and returns one row per creator with the contact that creator publishes. A free estimate shows the plan, the boundaries and the count before anything runs.

Audience Intel 根据您描述的目标受众，在 YouTube、TikTok、哔哩哔哩和抖音上找出这群人经常观看的创作者，按相关度排序，并给出每位创作者公开的联系方式，包括微信、QQ 或微博账号。运行前先显示免费预估。

What you get

  • Platforms: YouTube, TikTok, Bilibili, Douyin.
  • Contacts: email, website, a social profile that takes messages, and for Chinese creators WeChat, QQ or Weibo from the profile, or the profile page where they take bookings. Every contact says where it was found.
  • A creator with no way to reach them is not returned. Big channels with a single on-topic video are left out.
  • Measured: 19 of 20 TikTok creators returned come with an email.
  • Optional: a summary of each top creator’s latest videos, and a mailbox check on the emails.

How is this different from an influencer database?

A database is filtered by the creator’s own topic. This starts from your audience and returns the creators your people watch, whatever those creators are about.

What counts as a contact?

An email, a website, a social profile that takes messages, or a WeChat, QQ or Weibo ID.

Why not use the YouTube or TikTok API?

Neither gives a creator’s business email, and neither starts from an audience.

How to use them

How to run the apps: in your AI assistant, on Apify, or in n8n, Make or Zapier

All three ways need a free Apify account. The most common comes first. Steps checked against the Claude and OpenAI help pages on October 21, 2026.

In Claude (recommended)

  1. On claude.ai, open Customize → Connectors → Add → Add custom connector.
  2. Enter the address https://mcp.apify.com and sign in to Apify once.
  3. Ask in a sentence.

Custom connectors are available on Claude Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise; a free account can add one.

“Use the Lexonia Video Lens app on Apify to turn this Bilibili video into English with timestamps.”

In ChatGPT

Custom MCP server connections are available in ChatGPT Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces. A workspace admin or owner adds one under Plugins → Add custom MCP server, with the address https://mcp.apify.com. Personal plans (Free, Plus, Pro) are not included. If you are on a personal plan, use Claude.

Without an assistant

Open the app’s page on Apify and fill in the form. Or use Apify’s node in n8n, Make or Zapier to run an app on a schedule and send the results to Google Sheets, Slack or Notion.

Cursor, and Qwen or DeepSeek through tools such as Cherry Studio, connect to Apify the same way. Apify’s own page on the connector: mcp.apify.com.
