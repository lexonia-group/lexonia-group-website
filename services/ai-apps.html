Video Lens

Transcribe and translate videos from YouTube, TikTok, Reels, Bilibili, Douyin and Vimeo.

Paste a video link from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook Reels, Bilibili, Douyin or Vimeo, or a whole YouTube, Bilibili or Douyin channel. You get the transcript in its own language, a translation into any of 32 languages and a summary, plus subtitle files for YouTube, TikTok and Bilibili. No captions needed; it listens to the speech.

Why it matters. Much of what your market says lives in video, in a language you may not speak. Selling into China? Read what Chinese buyers watch on Bilibili and Douyin, in English. Working from China, or anywhere else? Read what Americans, Japanese or Italians are watching, in your own language. YouTube’s official API hands out caption files only for videos you are allowed to edit; Video Lens reads public videos on every platform above.

What people have used it for

  • A real estate company in London wanted the YouTube channels on travelling to and investing in Cyprus that UK and German travellers and investors watch. Audience Intel found the channels; Video Lens turned their videos into transcripts, the German ones translated into English, for the marketing team to build its strategy on.
  • An AI model developer got English summaries of what Chinese developers watch on Bilibili and Douyin about building local models on Qwen and DeepSeek.

Audience Intel

Find the creators your customers already watch, with a way to reach each one.

Describe your audience in your own words. You get the creators those people watch on YouTube, TikTok, Bilibili and Douyin, ranked, each with the contact they publish. A free estimate shows the search plan and the price before anything runs.

Why it matters. The creators your customers trust already have their attention. Reach out with the contact in hand and build a partnership: a co-marketing campaign, a sponsored video, or a revenue-share deal where the creator earns from every customer they send you. The app finds the creators; the deal is yours to shape.

What you can use it for

  • Hiring where the drivers already are. A mining company in northern Sweden needs truck drivers, and job boards keep sending the wrong people. The drivers it wants don’t read trucking news. They watch a handful of creators who film life on those roads. Audience Intel finds those creators and how to reach them, so the company can put its job ad inside their videos, in front of exactly the people it wants to hire.
  • Selling through people your customers trust. A skincare brand in the US is tired of paying for ads that get scrolled past. Its buyers learn their routines from a few YouTube and TikTok creators. Audience Intel ranks those creators, each with a business email, so the brand can offer them a revenue share: a code for their followers and a cut of every sale they bring in.

Business Finder

Built for lead generation: the businesses of one kind in a city, a state or a region, with phone and contact.

Searched in the local language too, so a search in Warsaw or Bangkok finds what an English search misses.

Why not just Google? Google Maps shows places one at a time on a map, for you to browse and copy by hand. Business Finder uses the same Google data and hands you the whole list as one table, searched in the local language, with the contact from each business’s own website, an optional mailbox check and reviews by period. Built for outreach, not browsing.

What you can use it for

  • Entering the US market. A medical device maker in South Korea has a new patient monitor cleared for sale in the US and no contacts there. It needs distributors, not hospitals. Business Finder lists the medical equipment suppliers in Texas, then state by state, each with phone, website and the contact from its own website. The sales team starts calling in week one instead of buying a list.
  • Winning bigger contracts. A landscaping company in Minnesota wants year-round work beyond homeowners. Commercial contracts come through property managers. Business Finder lists the property management companies across the Twin Cities, biggest first by number of reviews, each with phone, website and the contact from its own website. The owner calls the top twenty before the spring contracts are signed.

Reaching China

China has its own YouTube. Our apps open it up, in both directions.

A whole world of video you never see

Bilibili is China’s big video platform, and Douyin is the Chinese TikTok. Reviews, tutorials, travel, music, games, what people are buying and talking about: a whole culture lives there, in Chinese. Most Western tools stop at YouTube.

And they do not see ours

YouTube has been blocked in mainland China since 2009, and TikTok is not available there either. So for most people in China, the videos we watch are out of reach. If you want Chinese customers to find you, you go where they already watch.

How our apps help. Video Lens transcribes and translates Bilibili and Douyin videos. Audience Intel finds the creators Chinese buyers watch, with the WeChat, QQ or Weibo contact each one publishes.

What people have used it for

  • A product team watching the Chinese market got an English summary of creators’ reviews of a new phone on Bilibili and Douyin, with timestamps to the key moments.
  • A brand entering China found the Bilibili and Douyin creators that Chinese electric-car buyers watch, with WeChat, QQ or profile contacts.

How to run one

All three ways need a free Apify account. Steps checked on October 21, 2026.

1. In Claude

Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector. Enter https://mcp.apify.com, sign in to Apify once, then ask in a sentence: “Use Video Lens on Apify to turn this video into English.” A free Claude account can add one custom connector.

2. In ChatGPT

Available in Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces, added by an admin under Plugins → Add custom MCP server. Personal plans are not included; use Claude instead.

3. Without an assistant

Open the app on Apify and fill in the form. To run it on a schedule and send results to Google Sheets, Slack or Notion, use Apify’s node in n8n, Make or Zapier.
