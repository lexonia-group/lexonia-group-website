Meta campaigns built on your positioning

Ads on Facebook and Instagram written for the people already looking for what you do, not for a demographic. The whole path is wired in: the ad, a short form that qualifies the lead, a booking link, and the follow-up. A lead becomes an appointment without anyone copying it into a spreadsheet.

It is not a one-off campaign. Real lead data tells us what works and what to sharpen, and we report on it monthly.

Listeners for the questions people ask in public

Every day someone in a local group or on a forum like Reddit types “Can anyone recommend a good…?” Most businesses never see it. We set up listeners on those public conversations. When someone asks for exactly what you do, a message lands on your phone in Telegram, with the post and a link to it. You reply in your own words. Nothing goes out on its own, and nobody hears from us who did not ask.

Example. A business tells us the services it offers and the places it serves. We set the phrases its customers use, the groups and forums to watch, and where the alert goes. From then on the owner gets the question, not a list.

Questions

Where do the leads go?

A campaign lead lands in your Google Sheet and your inbox within seconds of the form being submitted, and can book a time straight into your calendar. A listener sends the post to your phone in Telegram.

Do you reply for me?

Listeners do not. The owner reads the message and replies in their own words. For lead platforms such as Thumbtack, an assistant can reply inside your rules; that is on the AI Automation page.

Ads or organic?

Both. Campaigns are paid and run on Facebook and Instagram. Listeners and your own social media cost nothing per lead.
