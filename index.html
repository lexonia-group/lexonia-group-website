The Problem

Why Most Local Ad Campaigns Don't Convert

Running ads without a locked positioning strategy is the most common — and most expensive — mistake local businesses make.

🎯

No Clear Positioning

If your ad copy can't explain why a client should choose you over every competitor, the algorithm can't find the right people either. Positioning isn't a tagline — it's the filter every campaign decision passes through.

📉

Targeting the Wrong Audience

Demographics aren't targeting. Knowing the psychographic portrait of your best clients — what they believe, want, and fear — is what makes Meta ads actually work at scale.

🔁

Campaigns, Not Systems

A single ad is a gamble. A positioning-led system — ad copy, funnel, lead form, automated follow-up — compounds into a predictable and repeatable lead flow month after month.

What We’ve Built for Clients

Three examples of problems we’ve solved — and the systems we built to solve them.

🔍 Influencer Discovery
Case 01 · Midwest Logistics

Finding 40 Opinion Leaders Out of Millions of Channels

A Midwest logistics company wanted to move beyond Meta ads and reach their customers through voices they already trusted. The challenge: finding the right YouTubers and TikTokers at scale, not by gut feel.

Using AI automation, we sifted through millions of social media accounts, scored each against the client’s target audience profile, and surfaced 40 high and medium-impact creators. We then developed a full partnership system: rev-share structure, content guidelines, and operational controls so the client could launch and manage creator relationships at scale.

What we delivered

40 vetted, ranked creator channels — plus a full rev-share & operational control framework ready to activate

⚡ AI Automation
Case 02 · Staffing & Recruitment

Real-Time Opportunity Matching Delivered to Your Inbox

A recruiting firm was losing placements because new job postings were found too late. Manual scanning didn’t scale. By the time their team found a match, the window had already closed.

We built an automated flow using web scraping, API connectors, and workflow automation that monitors sources continuously and delivers matched opportunities the moment they appear — no manual searching required.

What we delivered

A live monitoring system that sends matched opportunities to the right person the instant they appear

Also applicable to
Real estate agents Sales & prospecting Legal & compliance Grant writers E-commerce buyers Investors & VCs
📱 Social Media & Local Presence
Case 03 · Minneapolis Southwest Metro

Building a Digital Presence From the Ground Up

A multi-location taekwondo school in the Minneapolis southwest metro came to us with no meaningful digital presence and no clear positioning — just a great school with exceptional owners and a very engaged parents’ community, but with no way to communicate what made it different and why kids loved learning TKD with their masters.

We developed their positioning statement, built a content strategy around it, and launched consistent social media management. Alongside that, we set up and optimized their Google Business Profile and built a structured review management process — turning satisfied students and parents into a visible, growing reputation online.

What we delivered

Positioning, content strategy, social media management, GBP setup & review management — a consistent voice and a credible local presence
