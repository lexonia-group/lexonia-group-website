The Problem

Showing Up Online Waits for You

In a small business every post, reply, lead and decision waits for one person. When that person is busy, asleep or with family, the presence goes quiet and the leads go cold.

📱

Posting Waits for You

You know you should post three times a week. It becomes once a month, then nothing. A quiet page tells a prospect the business is quiet too.

📬

Leads Wait for You

A new inquiry lands at 9 pm. You see it at 7 am. The first good reply usually wins the customer, and it was not yours.

🛑

Automation You Can’t Trust

The fear is fair: a machine embarrassing you in front of a customer. So the rule has to be built in: nothing goes out on your behalf without your yes, and nothing it cannot verify goes out at all.

How It Works

Set Up Once. Runs Every Week. Approved by You.

This is not software you install. It is set up for your business on your positioning, runs under our watch, and reports to your phone.

01

Lock the Positioning

Who you are for, what they compare you to, why they choose you. Every post and every reply is written from it, in your voice.

02

Set Up the Pipeline

Your channels, your voice samples, your rules on what may never go out. Content is drafted weekly; leads route where you want them.

03

You Approve from Your Phone

Read the week’s batch, drop what you do not like, tap yes on the rest. Anything unsourced or unusual is held and shown to you instead of sent.

04

It Runs, We Watch

Posts go out on schedule, leads get answered, and you get a weekly note: what ran, what was held, what needs a decision.

🔍 Influencer Discovery
Case 01 · Midwest Logistics

Finding 40 Opinion Leaders Out of Millions of Channels

A Midwest logistics company wanted to move beyond Meta ads and reach their customers through voices they already trusted. The challenge: finding the right YouTubers and TikTokers at scale, not by gut feel.

Using AI automation, we sifted through millions of social media accounts, scored each against the client’s target audience profile, and surfaced 40 high and medium-impact creators. We then developed a full partnership system: rev-share structure, content guidelines, and operational controls so the client could launch and manage creator relationships at scale.

What we delivered

40 vetted, ranked creator channels — plus a full rev-share & operational control framework ready to activate

Case 02 · Staffing & Recruitment

Real-Time Opportunity Matching Delivered to Your Inbox

A recruiting firm was losing placements because new job postings were found too late. Manual scanning didn’t scale. By the time their team found a match, the window had already closed.

We built an automated flow using web scraping, API connectors, and workflow automation that monitors sources continuously and delivers matched opportunities the moment they appear — no manual searching required.

What we delivered

A live monitoring system that sends matched opportunities to the right person the instant they appear

Thumbtack Leads Answered While He Is on a Job
