Privacy Policy

How Lexonia Group collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective Date: June 2026  ·  Lexonia Group LLC  ·  Eden Prairie, MN

Information We Collect

When you contact us through this website, we collect information you voluntarily provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, business name, and details about your marketing situation. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and prepare for any scheduled consultation.

We also collect basic anonymous website usage data (pages visited, device type, approximate location) through standard analytics tools to understand how our site is being used and to improve it. This data is not linked to your identity.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to follow up on your inquiry, schedule strategy sessions, and prepare relevant market research before we connect. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Anonymous usage data is used only to improve our website experience.

Cookies & Third-Party Services

This website uses standard third-party tools for analytics and advertising measurement. These tools may place cookies in your browser and collect anonymous information about your visit. They are widely used across the internet and operated under their own privacy policies.

Scheduling links on this site redirect to a third-party booking platform. Any information you provide when scheduling a call is governed by that platform’s own privacy policy.

You can opt out of interest-based advertising through your browser settings or through the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have submitted to us. To make a request, email us at info@lexoniagroup.com. We will respond within 30 days.

California residents may request information about our data practices under the CCPA. We do not sell personal information. Canadian users may contact us for information about our PIPEDA compliance.

You may opt out of future marketing emails at any time by replying “unsubscribe” or contacting us directly.

Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this policy periodically. Changes take effect when posted on this page. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance.
