Service 01 · Social Media, Managed or Automated

Social Media Management & Automation

Two ways to have your channels handled, same positioning underneath. Managed: we plan, write, design and post for you, month after month, and report on it. Automated: your channels run from a weekly batch drafted in your voice; you read it on your phone, drop what you do not like, approve the rest with a tap, and we schedule what you approved.

A hold-back gate sits in front of everything: a post that names a number, a client or a result without a source on file is held and shown to you instead of sent. Your input is optional: send a photo or a sentence from your day and it goes into the next batch; an empty week still gets a full batch.

This is how we run our own publication: it has researched, written, checked and published every day for months without a person in the loop, and it holds back what it cannot source. For your channels, we schedule the approved posts by hand today; automatic publishing follows once the platform connections are approved.

What's Included

  • Managed option: monthly calendar, copy, creative and posting done for you
  • Automated option: weekly batch drafted in your voice and positioning
  • Approval from your phone: one tap per post
  • Hold-back gate: no unsourced number, name or claim goes out
  • Approved posts scheduled to your channels by us
  • Your photo or sentence goes into the next batch, when you send one
  • A weekly note: what posted, what was held, and why
  • Positioning-aligned copy, every week

Under All Three · Foundation

Positioning & Strategy

Every Lexonia engagement — an assistant, a campaign or a content pipeline — begins here. We define who you are, who you're for, and why you win before we touch a single ad.

The positioning statement has three elements: your Target Audience (psychographic portrait, not demographics), your Frame of Reference (the emotional world your customer is already in), and your Point of Difference (specific, customer-verified, hard to copy).

When all three are locked, they form the filter for every subsequent decision. Every ad, every funnel step, every automated follow-up is measured against it. If it doesn't follow from the positioning — it doesn't get built.

"Nothing gets built before the positioning is locked. An assistant that does not know why customers choose you cannot speak for you."

What's Included

  • Competitive intelligence analysis of your local market
  • Customer review mining (your reviews + top competitors)
  • Target Audience psychographic portrait
  • Frame of Reference definition
  • Point of Difference statement
  • Reason to Believe (RTB) anchors
  • Full locked positioning statement
