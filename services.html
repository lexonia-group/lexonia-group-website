The Non-Negotiable First Step · Foundation

Positioning & Strategy

Every Lexonia SMM engagement — regardless of which services you select — begins here. We define who you are, who you're for, and why you win before we touch a single ad.

The positioning statement has three elements: your Target Audience (psychographic portrait, not demographics), your Frame of Reference (the emotional world your customer is already in), and your Point of Difference (specific, customer-verified, hard to copy).

When all three are locked, they form the filter for every subsequent decision. Every ad, every funnel step, every automated follow-up is measured against it. If it doesn't follow from the positioning — it doesn't get built.

"No campaign brief leaves the desk without a locked positioning statement."

  • Competitive intelligence analysis of your local market
  • Customer review mining (your reviews + top competitors)
  • Target Audience psychographic portrait
  • Frame of Reference definition
  • Point of Difference statement
  • Reason to Believe (RTB) anchors
  • Full locked positioning statement

When a new lead comes in, Lexonia AI sends an immediate, personalized reply written in your voice. It qualifies the customer and keeps the exchange going — without you needing to stop what you’re doing.
